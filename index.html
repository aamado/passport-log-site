Passport Log

Counts your days in every country, automatically.

Your iPhone already knows when you cross a border. Passport Log turns that into a day-by-day record of the countries you have been in — for watching tax residency thresholds, building a residency or citizenship history, or simply keeping track of where you have been.

How it works

  • Records your country in the background, using a low-power service that wakes the app only when you move a significant distance.
  • Works out the country on the phone itself, from a map of borders built into the app, so it works with no signal and no roaming data.
  • Counts any part of a day, in the local time zone of wherever you were.
  • Fills in quiet days, and asks you about days where the country changed while it wasn't looking.
  • Warns you before you reach a limit you set, such as 183 days.
  • Exports your history as a CSV file for your accountant or your own records.

Your data stays yours

There is no server and no account. Everything stays on your iPhone and, if you want, in your own private iCloud. Nothing is sent to the developer, and there is no advertising or tracking of any kind.

Free

Passport Log is free, with no ads and nothing to buy.
