Privacy Policy

Passport Log · Last updated 23 September 2026

Passport Log does not collect any of your data. The app has no server, no account and no analytics. Everything it records stays on your iPhone and, if you leave iCloud sync on, in your own private iCloud storage, which only you can read.

Location

The app asks for location access so it can tell which country you are in. Location readings are processed entirely on your iPhone, using a map of country borders built into the app, so no network connection is involved and nothing is sent anywhere. Each reading is saved on your device with the time, the country it falls in and your time zone, so day counts can be recalculated later.

You can refuse location access, or choose "While Using the App" instead of "Always". The app still works, recording only when you open it, and you can add days by hand.

iCloud

If iCloud sync is on, your recorded days are stored in the private database of your own iCloud account so they survive a lost or replaced iPhone. This uses Apple's iCloud service under your Apple Account, and the developer has no access to it. You can turn sync off in the app's Settings.

What is never collected

  • No personal information, email address or account is requested.
  • No advertising, tracking or analytics software of any kind is included.
  • Nothing is shared with, or sold to, third parties.
  • No data is transmitted to the developer, who therefore cannot see where you have been.

Notifications

Warnings about day limits you set are scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is used, so the content of a warning never leaves your device.

Exporting and deleting

You can export your day history as a CSV file at any time, and choose yourself where to send it. The app's Settings also include Delete all data, which removes every recorded day from your iPhone and, when sync is on, from your iCloud.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
