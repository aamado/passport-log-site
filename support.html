Passport Log — Support

Counts your days in each country, automatically.

Passport Log records which country you are in and counts the days, for anyone who needs to know: digital nomads watching tax residency thresholds, people building a residency or citizenship history, and travellers keeping a record of where they have been.

Getting started

Open the app and choose Always when asked about location. iOS asks in two steps: first "While Using the App", then "Always". Only "Always" lets the app notice a country change while it is closed. Without it the app still works, but only records when you open it.

Why does a day say "Filled in"?

Staying in one place produces no new location readings, which is how the app stays light on battery. When the country is the same before and after a quiet stretch, those days are filled in automatically and marked Filled in.

Why does a day say "Needs review"?

If the country changed across a stretch with no readings — for example a flight with the phone switched off — the app cannot tell which day you crossed. Those days are marked Needs review and are left out of your totals until you tap one on the Today screen and choose the country.

How are days counted?

Any part of a day counts. If you drive from Portugal into Spain, that day counts for both, so totals can add up to more than the number of days in a period. Days are counted in the local time zone of wherever you were.

Adding past trips or fixing a day

On the Calendar screen, tap any day to add or remove a country by hand. The + button adds a past trip from before you installed the app: choose a country and the dates.

Day limits

In Settings, add a limit such as 183 days for a country, counted over a calendar year or the last 12 months. The app shows how many days are left, the date you would reach the limit if you stayed, and warns you 30 and 10 days before, and on the day.

Exporting your history

Settings → Export day history produces a CSV file with one row per day and country, including whether each day was recorded, filled in or added by hand.

iCloud and your data

Your days are kept on your iPhone and, unless you turn sync off in Settings, in your own private iCloud. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server. Settings also has Delete all data if you want to clear everything.

Battery

The app uses a low-power location service that wakes it only when you move a significant distance, rather than tracking you continuously. If it appears high in Settings → Battery, get in touch.

Still stuck?

Email antonio@amados.pt and describe what happened. Mentioning your iPhone model and iOS version helps.

Day counts are a good-faith record, not legal proof or tax advice. Rules differ between countries: some count any part of a day, others only days you were present at midnight or stayed overnight. Check your own case with a qualified adviser. · Privacy Policy
